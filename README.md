# Advanced Banking System Fivem ESX/QB/QBX Framework

**The ultimate banking experience for FiveM servers.** This script completely revamps the economy of your server with a sleek, interactive UI, a comprehensive credit score and loan system, dynamic stock market, physical ATM cards, and tax mechanics. Powered by `ak47_lib`, it features multi-framework support, shared accounts, and deep admin/police integrations.

## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 💳 Physical Cards & ATMs

  * **Card Tiers:** Choose from Basic, Gold, Platinum, or Black cards, each with daily limits and maintenance fees.
  * **ATM Minigames & PINs:** Insert your physical card into ATMs, enter your PIN, and interact with immersive camera angles and animations. Beware—entering the wrong PIN too many times will swallow the card\!

### 📈 Stock Market & Crypto

  * **Dynamic Trading:** Invest in dynamic stocks (like Fleeca, Maze Bank, LSC) and QubitCoin (Crypto).
  * **Realistic Market Mechanics:** Features volatility, bull/bear regimes, FOMO pumps, whale resistance, market crashes, and even company restructuring.

### 🏦 Loans & Credit Score

  * **Credit System:** Every player has a dynamic credit score that influences loan interest rates. Paying loans on time boosts the score, while missing payments penalizes it.
  * **Admin Underwriting:** Apply for personal, vehicle, or business loans. Admins can review, approve, or deny applications directly from city funds.

### 💼 Accounts & Taxes

  * **Account Types:** Full support for Personal, Shared (add/remove trusted users), and Society/Gang accounts.
  * **Taxes:** Automated wealth tax and income tax systems to act as an economy sink, complete with police flagging for tax evasion.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

  * **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, and various target systems (ox\_target, qb-target, qtarget).
  * **Admin & Police Dashboards:** Built-in panels for police to audit accounts and for admins to manage the city treasury and oversee the entire economy's circulation.
  * **Discord Logs:** Highly detailed, configurable Discord webhook logging for transactions, system actions, and admin overrides.

## ⚙️ How To Install:

  * Download ak47\_banking from your cfx portal.
  * Add the script to your resources folder.
  * Add the card items based on your framework instructions.
  * Add item images into your inventory.
  * Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
  * Make sure you have all dependencies.
  * Restart the server.
